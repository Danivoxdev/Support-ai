--- SECTION: GETTING STARTED ---

Q: How do I get started with Safari Funded?
A: Getting started is simple: 1) Pick an account size that matches your trading style, 2) Pay the one-time evaluation fee to start your Challenge, 3) Trade and hit the profit target while staying within the drawdown rules, 4) Once you pass, you receive a funded account, 5) Trade the funded account and request payouts once you're eligible. You can start your Challenge directly from the Safari Funded website.

Q: How do I sign up?
A: Head to safarifunded.com, choose your account size, and click "Start Challenge" to begin the signup and payment process.

Q: What happens after I pay for a challenge?
A: You get access to your evaluation trading account immediately. You can start trading right away, working toward the profit target while following the trading rules.

--- SECTION: TRADING RULES & PROHIBITED PRACTICES (REAL, from safarifunded.com) ---

These rules apply to BOTH evaluation phases and funded accounts. Safari Funded looks
for risk-conscious traders who bring their own systems and strategies. Speculative
trading strategies are welcomed. The practices below are considered serious violations
and result in IMMEDIATE ACCOUNT TERMINATION:

1. Arbitrage Trading — Exploiting price discrepancies or glitches across different
   markets involving similar or identical assets is strictly forbidden.

2. High-Frequency Trading (HFT) — Executing trades where most positions are held for
   a few seconds or less is prohibited, EXCEPT on EA account types.

3. Bulk Trading — Opening multiple trades simultaneously (manually or via automated
   tools) in a manner that clearly indicates no active trader involvement is not permitted.

4. News Bracketing — Placing pending buy and sell stop orders around the price ahead
   of high-impact news events is prohibited.

5. System Exploitation — Intentionally or unintentionally using strategies that take
   advantage of platform errors, including inaccurate or delayed price displays.

6. Copy Trading & Trade Coordination — Coordinating trades with other traders or
   accounts, including sharing signals/strategies to replicate positions across
   multiple accounts, is strictly forbidden.

7. One-Sided Betting — Consistently entering positions in a single direction
   regardless of market conditions or contrary indicators.

8. Prohibited EA Usage — EAs that scalp during rollover periods, or third-party EAs
   where multiple traders hold identical positions simultaneously, are not allowed.

9. Tick Scalping — Rapidly entering and exiting positions within seconds based on
   minor tick-level price fluctuations is prohibited.

10. Hedge & Reverse Arbitrage — Simultaneously buying and selling the same instrument
    across different accounts to exploit temporary pricing inefficiencies.

11. Account Sharing, Reselling & Management Services — Sharing, reselling, or letting
    another person/entity manage your account (including "pass your challenge"
    services) is strictly prohibited.

12. Cross-Operator Coordinated Trading — Entering opposite positions across accounts
    held with different providers/prop firms for manipulation purposes.

13. Artificial Profit Distribution — Strategies designed to spread profit across
    multiple days without proportionally distributing market risk (e.g. holding
    opposing positions on correlated instruments, partially closing the same trade
    idea across multiple days).

14. Server Overload via Automation — Automated robots/EAs generating excessive server
    requests through rapid opening, modification, or closing of trades/pending orders.

15. Inconsistent Position Sizing — Positions substantially larger/smaller than typical
    trading activity, or repeatedly concentrating risk in one instrument/correlated
    group beyond acceptable exposure levels.

16. Overleveraging — Overexposure to a single instrument or correlated group in a
    manner inconsistent with reasonable risk management standards.

CONSEQUENCES OF VIOLATION: Safari Funded may, at its sole discretion: immediately
terminate the trader relationship; suspend/block/restrict platform access; withhold
pending refunds or profit payouts; and/or permanently ban the trader from the platform.
